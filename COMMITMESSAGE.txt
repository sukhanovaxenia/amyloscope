feat(pipeline): domain overlap at Pfam-name depth by default

compute_domain_overlap() was called with its defaults, which group domains by
CATEGORY. At category depth the FXR1 architecture collapses to five buckets and
the KH_1 pair, the Agenet/Tudor pair and the three FXR_C domains each become one
box -- so a 2.97x enrichment in Agenet/Tudor and a near-total exclusion from
FXR_C were both being averaged into their neighbours.

group_by="name" separates Tudor_FXR1, Agenet, KH_9, KH_1, FXMRP1_C_core and
FXR_C1/C2/C3, and the unannotated residues appear as their own group so the
categories partition the sequence rather than covering part of it.

draws=2000 is stated rather than left to the default because the enrichment null
is what the p-values come from, and the figure is drawn from the same object as
the report -- recomputing it in the plotting layer could disagree with the text.

This changes what every existing domain_overlap.txt means. Regenerate rather
than compare.
